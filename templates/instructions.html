How to use Medi-Bot

  1. Register an account, then log in.
  2. Open the chatbot from the home page and tell it your name and age.
  3. Choose 1. Predict Disease and list the symptoms you are experiencing (see the symptoms list), or choose 2. Check Disease Symptoms and enter a disease name.
  4. The bot suggests a possible condition and links to nearby hospitals.

Important: Medi-Bot is a student demo project, not a medical device. Its predictions are rough guesses from a small dataset — always consult a qualified doctor for diagnosis or treatment.
